About Me

I write from the intersection of order and ache.
My work moves between fragments, reflection, and pharmaceutics not as escape, but as integration.

I’m interested in how discipline restores clarity, how the body carries truth, and how healing begins when we stop anesthetizing discomfort and learn to listen again. I write slowly, often after silence, often after resistance.

This space is for thoughts that don’t rush to resolve themselves:
for language that steadies, for systems that heal, for beauty that returns only when it’s honest.

Nothing here is written to perform.
Everything here is written to remain.
