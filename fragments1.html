Fragments

Human consciousness is not an accidental byproduct of the universe. Creation is not finished — it continues through awareness.
The desire to form something permanent arrives differently for each soul.
Be not godless in your hearts. — Enoch
YHWH Awaken me again.
Do not invite someone into a life you are not yet proud of.
Anger is a familiar taste. I do not like it — yet sometimes I feast.
Love — divine, yes, yet here I remain human.
History sometimes calls upon the heart when intellect surrenders.
Faith Relish the mystery.
There is delight in dancing on the edge.
Worship imitates what it exalts.
Draw not wisdom from the unwise.
We empty ourselves to sustain another.
Meditation requires long affection of the mind.
We are here for a season. Represent Him well.
The spirit of your aim answers your prayers.
Not all purpose is romantic.
You cannot bind me with false feeling.
